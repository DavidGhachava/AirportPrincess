Your First Flight Made Easy

A step-by-step guide for going from Los Angeles to Istanbul, then Istanbul to Tbilisi — without having to guess what to do.

Departure LAX 🇺🇸 Los Angeles
✈️ TK180
Connection IST 🇹🇷 Istanbul

❤️ First: Don't Panic

Part 1

🛫 Getting to LAX

When you arrive at Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), you need to find the Turkish Airlines check-in area.

Look for the large airport screens showing:

Departures / Departures & Gates

Find:

TK180 — Istanbul (IST)

The screen will tell you the current check-in area, terminal/zone and eventually your gate.

Go to the Turkish Airlines check-in desks.

Part 2

🎫 Check-in

At the Turkish Airlines counter, have your passport, flight reservation/booking, any required travel documents and phone ready.

Tell the employee:

“I am flying to Istanbul and then connecting to Tbilisi.”

They will check your passport and reservation.

You may receive one or two boarding passes.

Ideally, because your whole journey is on the same Turkish Airlines itinerary, you should receive your boarding pass for TK180 and, if available, your connecting boarding pass for TK384.

They will also take your checked suitcase if you have one.

🧳 Very important — your checked bag

If you check a suitcase, look at the baggage tag they put on it.

Your final destination should normally be:

TBS — Tbilisi

if the entire journey is booked as one connected itinerary.

Ask the employee:

“Is my luggage checked all the way to Tbilisi?”

Wait for them to confirm.

⚠️ Important: If they say yes, you do NOT collect your checked suitcase in Istanbul. If they say no, follow their instructions.
Part 3

🛂 Security at LAX

After checking in, you need to go through airport security.

Look for signs saying:

Security
or
TSA Security

Follow the signs.

There will probably be a line.

When you reach the security checkpoint, follow the instructions from the TSA officers.

You may need to:

  • Put your carry-on bag on the conveyor belt
  • Put certain electronics/items into a tray
  • Remove items from your pockets
  • Go through a scanner
  • Collect everything on the other side

The exact procedure can change, so just do what the TSA officer tells you.

💡 Tip: Don't worry if everyone seems to know what they're doing. You can simply copy the general process of the people in front of you.

🚨 Before walking away from security

PHONE ✅
PASSPORT ✅
WALLET ✅
BAG ✅
HEADPHONES ✅

Your passport is the most important thing you have.

Part 4

🏢 You are now airside

Once you pass security, you are inside the secure part of the airport.

Now you need to find your GATE.

❓ What is a gate?

A gate is basically the specific airport area where passengers wait before boarding their airplane.

Think of it like this:

Airport → Terminal → Gate → Airplane

For example:

Gate 52 → wait there → TK180 boards → walk onto airplane

🖥️ How to find your gate

Find a large screen showing:

DEPARTURES

Find:

TK180

Then look across the same row.

You should see information such as:

TK180 — ISTANBUL — GATE XX — BOARDING XX:XX
⚠️ The gate can change.
That is NORMAL.

If your boarding pass says Gate 60 but the airport screen later says Gate 52:

FOLLOW THE AIRPORT SCREEN.

Airport screens are the latest information.

Part 5

🪑 Waiting at the gate

Once you find the gate, check the screen near the gate.

It should show information for your flight.

You can sit down and wait.

You do NOT need to stare at the airplane the entire time.

Listen for announcements and check the screen occasionally.

🚪 What does "boarding" mean?

Boarding = getting onto the airplane.

When boarding begins, passengers line up near the gate.

You will show your boarding pass + passport to the staff.

They scan/check your boarding pass.

Then you follow the signs/staff toward the airplane.

Sometimes you walk directly through a tunnel called a jet bridge.

Sometimes a bus takes passengers from the terminal to the airplane.

💡 Don't worry about knowing which one. Just follow the airport staff and the other passengers.
Part 6

✈️ Getting on TK180

When you enter the airplane, look at your boarding pass.

It will have your:

Seat number

For example:

24A

This means:

Row 24 + Seat A

There will be numbers above the seats showing the rows.

Walk down the airplane until you reach your row.

If you're confused, ask a flight attendant:

“Excuse me, could you help me find seat 24A?”

They will help you.

Put your carry-on in the overhead compartment if appropriate, or under the seat in front of you.

Then sit down.

🎧 On the plane

Once everyone is seated, the crew will demonstrate the safety instructions.

You don't need to memorize everything.

Just pay attention to:

  • Seat belt
  • Emergency exits
  • Oxygen masks
  • Safety card

Keep your seat belt fastened whenever you're seated, especially when the seat-belt sign is on.

Part 7

🌎 The 12+ hour flight to Istanbul

TK180 is the long part of the journey.

The flight will be many hours, so don't expect it to feel like a normal short flight.

You can:

  • Sleep
  • Watch movies
  • Use your phone when permitted
  • Listen to music
  • Eat the meals provided
  • Drink water
  • Walk around occasionally when the seat-belt sign is off

There will be toilets on the airplane.

If you want to use one, you can simply get up when the seat-belt sign is off and go.

If the sign is on, stay seated unless there is an emergency.

💺 Don't worry about the airplane moving

You will hear strange noises.

You may feel the airplane:

  • Shake
  • Vibrate
  • Turn
  • Go up/down slightly
  • Make loud engine sounds
  • Make noises during takeoff and landing
❤️ That is normal.
The aircraft is designed for this.
Part 8

🇹🇷 Arriving in Istanbul

THIS IS THE PART THAT SOUNDS SCARY BUT IS ACTUALLY SIMPLE.

You land at:

Istanbul Airport — IST

You have approximately 4 hours before your Tbilisi flight.

You are NOT finished with your journey.

You still need to find:

TK384 — Tbilisi

🚨 Very important

Do NOT automatically follow “EXIT / BAGGAGE CLAIM / ARRIVALS.”

You are connecting to another international flight.

You want to follow signs that say:

🔵 INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

or

🔵 TRANSFER / TRANSIT
Part 9

🛂 Do we go through Turkey immigration?

Because Istanbul is only your connection and your destination is Tbilisi, you normally follow the international transfer route rather than entering Turkey.

You should not go toward baggage claim or Turkish arrivals unless airport staff specifically directs you there.

There may still be another security check for connecting passengers.

If there is:

Follow the line → put your belongings through security → collect them → continue following TRANSFER signs.

Your exact route depends on the airport's current procedures.

Part 10

🧭 Finding TK384

Once you're in the transfer area, look for the big airport screens again.

Find:

TK384 — TBILISI

The screen should show the current:

Gate

and eventually:

Boarding time
⭐ THE GOLDEN RULE

Your flight number is more important than your gate number.

Always search for:

TK384

Not just “Tbilisi.”

Not just “Georgia.”

Not just a gate number.

Find TK384 on the departure board.

Part 11

🚶 Walk to the gate

Istanbul Airport is HUGE.

You may have to walk quite a long distance.

That's completely normal.

Follow signs toward the gate shown for TK384.

You may see signs such as:

A →
B →
C →
F →

These are different gate areas.

Follow the signs corresponding to your gate.

If the screen says:

TK384 — Gate F12

follow:

F →

Then:

F12

⏰ You have 4 hours

Four hours is a comfortable amount of time for a normal international connection.

But don't think:

“I have four hours, I'll explore the entire airport first.”

Instead:

FIRST: Find your gate.

Once you know where your gate is, THEN you can relax.

You can get food, use the bathroom, charge your phone, walk around, etc.

But keep an eye on the departure screens.

Part 12

🔔 How do you know when to board?

There are several ways.

1️⃣ The screen

The departure screen will change.

BOARDING

or

FINAL CALL

2️⃣ Announcements

The airport may announce the flight.

3️⃣ The gate screen

The screen directly at your gate will show information for TK384.

4️⃣ Your phone

Turkish Airlines may provide notifications through its app/booking system if notifications are enabled.

🚨 DO NOT RELY ONLY ON YOUR PHONE.

The airport screens are your main source of truth.

🚨 What if the gate changes?

Don't panic.

This happens.

Imagine you are sitting at Gate F12.

Then the airport screen changes:

TK384 → Gate F18

That's not a disaster.

Simply follow the signs to F18 and go there.

Always check the screens periodically.

Part 13

✈️ Boarding TK384

When TK384 starts boarding:

Take:

Passport + boarding pass

and go to the gate.

The staff will check/scan your boarding pass.

Then you board the airplane.

Find your seat exactly like you did on TK180.

Put your carry-on away.

Sit down.

Seat belt.

You're on the final flight. 🇬🇪
Part 14

🇬🇪 Arriving in Tbilisi

After landing in Tbilisi, leave the airplane.

Now you are actually entering Georgia.

Follow signs for:

ARRIVALS

PASSPORT CONTROL / IMMIGRATION

You will go through Georgian border control.

Have your passport ready.

Follow the instructions of the border officers.

After passport control, follow signs for:

BAGGAGE CLAIM

If you checked a suitcase all the way to Tbilisi, find the baggage carousel for your flight.

The airport screens will tell you which carousel to use.

Wait for your suitcase.

Check the luggage tag if you're unsure that it's yours.

🧳 If your bag doesn't appear

Don't panic.

Go to the airline's baggage service desk and show them:

  • Passport
  • Baggage tag
  • Boarding pass

Tell them:

“My checked luggage hasn't arrived.”

They can locate it and tell you what happens next.

Part 15

🚪 Leaving the airport

After collecting your luggage, follow:

EXIT

You will eventually reach the public arrivals area.

🎉 That's it!

You made it from Los Angeles all the way to Tbilisi.

🧠 Remember

The only things you really need to remember

If everything becomes confusing, remember these three rules:

RULE #1 — FOLLOW THE SIGNS

At LAX:
CHECK-IN → SECURITY → GATE

At Istanbul:
TRANSFER → INTERNATIONAL TRANSFER → TK384 → GATE

At Tbilisi:
PASSPORT CONTROL → BAGGAGE CLAIM → EXIT

RULE #2 — FOLLOW YOUR FLIGHT NUMBER

Your two flight numbers are:

✈️ TK180
LAX → IST

then

✈️ TK384
IST → TBS

If you're lost, find an airport screen and look for the flight number.

RULE #3 — ASK FOR HELP

There is absolutely nothing embarrassing about asking.

Show your boarding pass and say:

“I have TK384 to Tbilisi. Where do I go?”

An airport employee will understand immediately.

You don't need perfect English.

You don't need to know the airport.

You don't need to understand every sign.

You just need to find the next step.

❤️ Final message

You're not going to be dropped into a giant airport and told:

“Good luck, figure it out.”

Airports are designed so millions of people can navigate them every day.

There are signs everywhere.

There are departure screens everywhere.

There are airport employees everywhere.

Your boarding pass tells you your flight.

Your flight number tells you what to look for.

And your connecting flight is already planned into your itinerary.
